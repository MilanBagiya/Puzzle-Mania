# Animal track guides

## Template (in this order)
1. Frontmatter: title (under 60 chars, e.g. "Fox Tracks in Snow: How to Identify Them"),
   description (under 155 chars), slug, animal, sources (list of URLs), updated date.
2. One-sentence answer as the first paragraph.
3. What the tracks look like (size in inches and cm, toes, claws, shape).
4. How it moves and what its trail looks like.
5. Look-alikes table (2 to 4 similar animals).
6. Where and when to find the tracks.
7. 3 quick facts.
8. Practice box linking to /fresh-snow.
9. Sources list.

## Fact rules (strict)
- Every number and fact must come from a wildlife agency, park service, university or
  similarly reliable source, listed in Sources.
- If you are not sure, mark the sentence [CHECK] instead of guessing.
- Never copy sentences from sources; write in your own plain words.
- Short sentences, no filler, 800 to 1,200 words.

The approved drafts for 12 guides are in docs/ (exported). Use them as the content;
convert to Markdown files, do not rewrite facts.
