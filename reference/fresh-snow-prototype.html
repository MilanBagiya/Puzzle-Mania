How to play

Last night, animals walked through the snow. Follow their footprints home.

  1. 1Pick an animal. Each one has its own colour of prints.
  2. 2Tap its prints in order, starting from the edge of the board.
  3. 3End at a home , the brown mound. Each home fits one animal.
Fox walks diagonally ↗
Rabbit hops 2 squares straight →→
Deer walks up, down, left or right ↑

White snow mounds can hide prints. Stuck? Tap Hint.
