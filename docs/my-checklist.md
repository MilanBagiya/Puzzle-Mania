# My Checklist

Your own tasks, the ones an AI can't do for you: decisions, accounts, money, testing with real people, marketing and review. Work through the phases in order; each ends with a gate to meet before moving on. The AI's coding tasks are in the AI Build Checklist tab.

## Phase 0 · Validate the idea (Nov 23 to Dec 6, 2026)

Prove people enjoy Fresh Snow on paper and lock in the name before writing much code.

**Test the idea**

- [ ] Draw 3 Fresh Snow puzzles by hand on paper (5 x 5, 2 to 3 animals).
- [ ] Test with 5 people who like puzzles; watch silently, note where they get confused.
- [ ] Ask each: "Would you play a new one tomorrow?" Write down exact answers.
- [ ] Adjust rules based on the 2 biggest confusions.

**Name and domain**

- [ ] Shortlist 5 names (start with Tracklore, Hollowlog, Pinewit).
- [ ] Run the 5-minute check for each (SEO Plan tab, Domain and name).
- [ ] Buy the winning .com at Cloudflare or Spaceship; turn on auto-renew, WHOIS privacy, 2FA, registrar lock.
- [ ] Reserve social handles on X, Instagram, TikTok and Reddit.

**Accounts and setup**

- [ ] Create GitHub and Cloudflare accounts with 2FA.
- [ ] Install Claude Code (or your AI coding tool) and put `CLAUDE.md` in the repo.
- [ ] Set aside fixed build hours each week (for example 2 evenings + weekend mornings).

**Gate**: 4 of 5 testers finish a paper puzzle and ask for another. If not, change the rules and retest before Phase 1.

## Phase 1 · Build the MVP (Dec 7, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027)

The AI writes most of the code (AI Build Checklist, Milestones 0 to 4); your job is to direct it, review it, test it and handle everything outside the code.

**Direct and review the AI**

- [ ] Give the AI one task at a time using the prompt templates; approve its plan before it codes.
- [ ] Run tests and play the result after every task; commit only what you understand.
- [ ] Solve 10 generated puzzles yourself each week; flag any that feel unfair.

**Art and content**

- [ ] Decide the art route: draw flat SVG animals yourself, or commission a set (budget up to $300).
- [ ] Get the 6 print shapes and 6 animal illustrations (fox, rabbit, deer, squirrel, bird, wolf).
- [ ] Write or review the 12 launch animal guides; check every fact against wildlife agency or park sources.
- [ ] Write the About page in your own words (who you are, why you made it).

**Legal and money**

- [ ] Short consult with a lawyer on privacy, cookies and children's pages (COPPA, UK Children's Code, GDPR, India's data law).
- [ ] Ask a chartered accountant how to declare ad income, whether GST registration applies, and how to fill in US tax information (form W-8BEN) in AdSense to claim treaty benefits.
- [ ] Set up a separate bank account or tracking sheet for project income and costs.
- [ ] Confirm the privacy policy and terms match what the site actually does.

**Ads and search accounts**

- [ ] Verify the domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the sitemap.
- [ ] Apply for Google AdSense once content pages and the privacy policy are live.
- [ ] Set up the free Google Privacy & messaging consent tool for EU/UK visitors.

**Gate**: 30 days of puzzles are ready, pages load under 2 s on your phone, and a friend finishes the tutorial and today's set without your help.

## Phase 2 · Private beta (Jan 4 to Jan 17, 2027)

Two weeks with 30 to 50 real players to find out whether people come back the next day, and fix what stops them.

**Recruit testers**

- [ ] Invite friends, family and colleagues who enjoy puzzles (aim for 20).
- [ ] Find 10 to 30 more from puzzle communities: ask in a friendly post, offer early access and credit on the About page.
- [ ] Aim for at least half of testers in the US, UK, Canada or Australia.
- [ ] Create a simple feedback form (Google Form or Tally) with the daily question: "Did you want tomorrow's puzzle?"

**Run the beta**

- [ ] Read every feedback response daily; keep a running list of issues ranked by how many people hit them.
- [ ] Check analytics daily: solves, quit points, day-1 return rate.
- [ ] Watch 3 new testers play over a video call; note where they hesitate.
- [ ] Have the AI fix the top 10 issues; retest each fix yourself.
- [ ] Tune difficulty bands from real solve times (Game Design tab, tuning table).

**Prepare the launch**

- [ ] Record 3 short GIFs: a puzzle being solved, the snowfall finish, the share card.
- [ ] Write launch posts for Reddit, Hacker News ("Show HN"), Product Hunt and directories.
- [ ] Read each community's self-promotion rules before posting.
- [ ] Prepare a list of 10 newsletters and 10 "games like Wordle" articles to contact.
- [ ] Ask beta testers if they'll share on launch day.

**Gate**: 40% or more of beta testers come back the next day. If lower, extend the beta a week and fix the biggest reason people leave.

## Phase 3 · Launch week (Jan 18 to Jan 24, 2027)

Launch on Monday, January 18, and spend the week answering every comment and fixing problems fast; first impressions decide who returns.

**Day before**

- [ ] Tick every box in the main plan's Final launch checklist.
- [ ] Remove the beta banner; confirm the generator buffer holds 30+ days.
- [ ] Clear your calendar for launch day.

**Launch day** (post during US morning, Eastern Time)

- [ ] Post on Reddit (one community at a time, spaced a few hours apart).
- [ ] Post "Show HN" on Hacker News with the generator and solver story.
- [ ] Launch on Product Hunt; ask beta testers to try it.
- [ ] Submit to daily-game directories (Listdle, wordlelike.games) and the Room Escape Artist guide.
- [ ] Reply to every comment within a few hours, kindly, including criticism.
- [ ] Watch Sentry and uptime alerts; fix anything broken the same day.

**Rest of the week**

- [ ] Email the 10 newsletters and 10 article authors with a GIF and one-line pitch.
- [ ] Post in nature and teacher communities about the animal guides.
- [ ] Note which channel brought the most returning players (not just visits).
- [ ] Write a short "launch week" summary for yourself: visits, day-1 return, top feedback.

## Phase 4 · Grow (Jan 25 to Apr 30, 2027)

Turn launch visitors into daily players and add the next game; this is where the habit and the audience get built.

**Product decisions**

- [ ] Each week, pick the one feature from AI Build Checklist Milestone 6 that most helps retention, and ship it.
- [ ] Check the Stepping Stones idea against existing games before the AI builds it; launch it in March.
- [ ] Approve the spring mud biome art and launch it in March.
- [ ] Keep or drop features based on data, not feelings (day-7 return, quit rates).

**Audience and outreach**

- [ ] Post each Sunday story as a social teaser.
- [ ] Announce every new game and biome on the same launch channels.
- [ ] Send 5 outreach emails a week (teachers, nature sites, list-article authors).
- [ ] Once daily players pass \~1,000, pitch game and culture writers with your real numbers.
- [ ] Start a small Discord or subreddit if players ask for one.

**Money**

- [ ] Check AdSense earnings weekly; confirm ads never slow the board or shift layout.
- [ ] Apply to Journey by Mediavine once you pass 1,000 monthly sessions.
- [ ] Record monthly income and costs in your tracking sheet.

**Gate**: steady growth in daily players for 4 weeks in a row, day-7 return at 25% or more, and Journey approved.

## Phase 5 · Scale to $1,000/month (May to Dec 2027)

Grow to about 150,000 pageviews a month from mostly US, UK, Canadian and Australian players, and move to a premium ad network.

**Product**

- [ ] Commission or draw 120+ original animal illustrations before Who Was Here? launches (May).
- [ ] Approve Family mode and Classroom mode designs; have a lawyer review the kids' pages before they go live.
- [ ] Launch summer sand (June) and autumn leaves (September) biomes.
- [ ] Add one new game every 6 to 8 weeks only if the last one keeps 30%+ of its players after 7 days.

**Audience**

- [ ] Back-to-school push (August to September): share Classroom mode with teachers and education sites.
- [ ] Offer the embeddable "puzzle of the day" widget to nature and education sites.
- [ ] Pitch press again with milestone numbers (for example 10,000 daily players).
- [ ] Plan a December "Year in the Wild" post and share cards.

**Money**

- [ ] Apply to Raptive or Playwire once past 25,000 monthly pageviews; run each for a month and keep the better RPM.
- [ ] Test one ad placement change at a time; keep it only if retention holds.
- [ ] Decide on the optional rewarded video for streak repair after seeing player feedback.
- [ ] Review taxes with your accountant before the financial year ends.

**Gate**: about $1,000 or more in monthly ad revenue for 2 months in a row.

## Ongoing routines

After launch, a steady rhythm of about 10 minutes a day, 1 hour a week and 2 hours a month keeps the hub healthy; these repeat, so leave them unticked as a reference.

**Daily (10 minutes)**

- Play today's set yourself; it's the fastest way to spot problems.
- Check alert emails (generator buffer, errors, uptime).
- Reply to player messages and comments.

**Weekly (1 hour)**

- Review dashboard: daily players, day-1 and day-7 return, quit rates, share rate, traffic by country, RPM.
- Play-test the coming Sunday Blizzard and one random weekday puzzle.
- Pick next week's one feature or fix for the AI.
- Send 5 outreach emails; post the Sunday story teaser.

**Monthly (2 hours)**

- SEO routine from the SEO Plan tab (Search Console, pages ranking 8 to 20, 2 new guides).
- Recalibrate difficulty against real solve times.
- Record income and costs; compare RPM against last month.
- Keep or drop each game by the 30%-return rule.
- Re-read this checklist and the roadmap; update dates if anything slipped.
