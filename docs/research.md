# Research

Research done in November 2026 shows daily puzzle demand is huge and growing, the winners are original games that grow through sharing, and a game built for US, UK, Canadian and Australian players earns several times more per visit than one built for Indian traffic. Traffic figures are third-party estimates (Semrush, Similarweb), not exact numbers.

## Market demand

Daily puzzles are a mass habit: "wordle" alone is searched about 43 million times a month in the US, and NYT's games were played 11.1 billion times in 2024.

| Signal | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "wordle" US monthly searches (Nov 2026) | \~43.45 million (5th most searched term in the US) | [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/top-google-searches/) |
| "wordle" global monthly searches | \~76 million | [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/top-google-searches/) |
| NYT Games plays in 2024 | 11.1 billion total: Wordle 5.3B, Connections 3.3B, Strands 1.3B | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Games) |
| Daily games listed in one directory | 1,313 "games like Wordle" | [wordlelike.games](https://wordlelike.games/) |
| LinkedIn daily games | 7 daily puzzles, crafted by a three-time world Sudoku champion | [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/heres-how-hes-shaping-linkedins-puzzle-game) |

**What it means**: demand is proven and still growing, especially for logic puzzles (LinkedIn's Queens, Tango, Zip; NYT's Pips), but simple Wordle-style clones face a crowded field.

## Proof it earns

Solo developers have built daily puzzle games that earn a full salary from ads, but results range widely: this is a hit-driven business, so plan to try more than one game.

| Game | Built by | Result | How it grew | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Actorle | One developer, built in 3 days | \~$4,000/month; ad revenue matched his salary and he quit his job | b3ta.com newsletter, Twitter shares, Mashable | [hey.gg](https://www.hey.gg/blog/actorle) |
| Clues by Sam | Small studio (Ad Artis Oy) | 50,000+ daily players within \~9 months | Word of mouth, sharing | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clues_By_Sam) |

## Ad market facts

Where players live matters more than anything else for ad income: the same pageview from the US can earn 5 to 10 times more than one from India.

**Page RPM by audience** (earnings per 1,000 pageviews)

| Audience | Page RPM | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| India, AdSense (general) | $1 to $3 | [Ranktracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/how-much-does-adsense-pay-for-1-000-views-in-india) |
| India, finance niche | ₹250 to ₹830 | [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/how-to-calculate-google-adsense-earnings-india/) |
| US, gaming and entertainment | $5 to $12 | [WeForAds](https://weforads.com/blog/ad-revenue-benchmarks-2026/) |
| US, general (AdSense) | $20 to $50 | [Ranktracker](https://www.ranktracker.com/blog/how-much-does-adsense-pay-for-1-000-views-in-india) |

**Ad network entry rules (2026)**

| Network | Entry requirement | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google AdSense | No traffic minimum | — |
| Journey by Mediavine | 1,000 monthly sessions; 70% revenue share; upgrades at $5,000 yearly ad revenue | [Jupiter](https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify) |
| Raptive | 25,000 monthly pageviews | [Eneba](https://www.eneba.com/hub/play-to-earn/how-do-i-make-money-with-a-blog/) |
| Playwire | Works with daily puzzle sites such as Flagdoku | [Playwire](https://www.playwire.com/case-studies/flagdoku) |

**AI Overviews**: a 2026 field experiment found Google's AI Overviews cut clicks by about 40% on queries where they appear, and small publishers lost around 60% of search traffic ([AI Growth Agent](https://aigrowthagent.co/articles/ai-overviews-traffic-impact/)). Simple answer pages lose most; interactive games can't be replaced by an AI summary.

**Children's ads**: on sites or sections directed at children under 13, Google requires child-directed tagging and no interest-based ads ([Google](https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10436800?hl=en)), which lowers earnings on those pages.

## Idea validation

Several directions were tested against the research; Fresh Snow was chosen because no daily web game built on reading animal tracks turned up, and it suits code-generated, language-free logic puzzles.

| Idea | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grow CleverTools (Indian tools site) | Not for ads-only income | Indian traffic earns \~$1 to $3 RPM; would need \~25,000 daily visitors for $1,000/month; generic tools compete with giants |
| Daily hint/answer pages for NYT games | Rejected | Owned by Forbes, Mashable, Yahoo, CNET; AI Overviews take the clicks; low $0.14 CPC |
| Another Wordle clone | Rejected | 1,300+ similar games already listed |
| Geography daily game | Rejected | Crowded: Worldle, Globle, Flagle, Travle and many copies |
| Dinner-party seating logic puzzle | Rejected | Already exists as daily web games (Seated, Seat Plan) and several apps (Is This Seat Taken?, Table Trouble, Seat Clue) |
| Countdown / "days until" pages | Backup only | Easy, but low engagement and low RPM |
| **Fresh Snow (animal tracks logic)** | **Chosen** | Searches found only kids' track-guessing books and nature lessons, no daily logic game; visual, global, calm, educational, fully code-generated |
| Nests, Stepping Stones (companions) | To verify | Built on known logic-puzzle types; check the twist and theme against existing games before building |

Recheck Fresh Snow's uniqueness and the final hub name (domain, trademarks, app and game listings) right before launch.

## Lessons that shaped the plan

Each major decision in the plan traces back to one finding.

| Finding | Decision in the plan |
| --- | --- |
| Winners get 57 to 74% of visits directly; Google is a small share | Growth through sharing, streaks and communities, not SEO first |
| Their search traffic is mostly their own name | Short, unique game and hub names; each game gets its own page |
| Clues by Sam's 10-minute visits beat Globle's 3.5 minutes | Deeper logic flagship + quick companions in one Daily Set |
| US/UK/AU traffic pays 5 to 10x Indian traffic | English, globally themed, launched to US/UK communities |
| "Unlimited" versions of daily games attract their own traffic | Practice mode and archive for every game |
| Hits are unpredictable; Twofer Goofer earned \~$7K a year, Actorle \~$4K a month | A hub of several games, adding one every 6 to 8 weeks |
| AI Overviews take clicks from answer pages | No answer pages; interactive games and helpful guides only |
| Premium networks now accept small sites | AdSense at launch, Mediavine Journey from 1,000 sessions |
| Kids' pages can't run interest-based ads | Kids join through Family and Classroom modes; main hub for adults and families |
