# Game Design Document

Wild Daily (working name) is a web-only daily puzzle hub with a shared forest world: Fresh Snow is the flagship, with smaller companion games added over time.

## Hub concept and daily set

Every day the hub offers a **Daily Set** of short puzzles in one shared forest world; finishing the set is the main daily goal. Everything runs in the browser: no app store, no download, no sign-up needed to play.

**Design pillars**

1. **Short, satisfying, daily.** Every game has one puzzle per day (or one per difficulty), a clear finish, and a reason to return tomorrow.
2. **One world.** All games share the forest theme, the Forest Journal, one streak and one share card.
3. **Logic first, language-light.** Mostly visual puzzles that work for players in any English-speaking country.
4. **Fair and kind.** One correct answer per puzzle, no luck, no pay-to-win, no guilt messages.
5. **Generated by code.** Every game's daily puzzles are produced and verified automatically.

**The Daily Set over time**

| Period | Games in the set | Typical time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch (Jan 2027) | Fresh Snow (Morning, Noon, Night) + Nests | 12 to 20 min |
| From March 2027 | + Stepping Stones | 15 to 23 min |
| From May 2027 | + Who Was Here? | 16 to 25 min |
| Later | One new game every 6 to 8 weeks, kept only if players return to it | — |

**A player's day**

1. Open the hub at any time after local midnight; the Today page shows the set, the streak and a countdown to the next snowfall.
2. Start with the quick games (Nests, Fresh Snow Morning) to warm up.
3. Finish with Fresh Snow Night (or the Sunday Blizzard).
4. See the result screen: times, percentile, Journal card earned, one share card for the whole set.
5. Optional: play the archive or practice mode.

## Fresh Snow: full rules

The player rebuilds every animal's overnight path from the prints left in the snow; a solution is correct when every path follows its animal's rule and every visible print is explained.

**Board elements**

| Element | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Snow cell | Walkable; may show prints |
| Covered cell (fresh drift) | Walkable; prints hidden |
| Tree | Blocks deer, fox and rabbit; birds and squirrels use it |
| Rock | Blocks all walking animals |
| Hideout (burrow, log, den, hollow) | Each animal ends in exactly one; one animal per hideout |
| Edge gap | Where an animal may enter the board |

**Prints**: each animal has a unique print shape. A print shows which animal stood there; a direction arrow appears only on harder levels' "clear print" cells. A crossing cell shows two shapes.

**Validity rules for a solution**

1. Every animal listed on today's cards has one path from an entry to a hideout.
2. Each step follows that animal's movement rule (see the plan's animal table).
3. No path passes through rocks; only birds and squirrels touch trees.
4. Every visible print belongs to a path that stands on that cell; no path stands on a visible empty snow cell (empty snow = nobody stood there).
5. Paths never revisit a cell, except wolves walking single file.

**Solving tools**

- Draw: tap an animal card, then tap cells in order; the path snaps to legal moves only.
- Pencil: mark a cell with an animal's small icon ("could be fox").
- Check: confirms whether current paths break any rule, without revealing the answer; checks are counted.
- Undo, redo, clear one animal, clear all.

**Difficulty profiles**

| Level | Grid | Animals | Covered cells | Crossings | Clear prints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | 5 x 5 | 2 | 0 to 2 | 0 to 1 | Many |
| Noon | 6 x 6 | 3 | 3 to 5 | 1 to 2 | Some |
| Night | 7 x 7 | 4 | 5 to 9 | 2 to 4 | Few |
| Sunday Blizzard | 8 x 8 | 5 to 6 | 9 to 14 | 4 to 6 | Very few |
| Cub (family) | 3 x 3 or 4 x 4 | 1 to 2 | 0 | 0 | All |

**New-mechanic rollout**: introduce one new animal or board element per week for the first two months, each with a 20-second interactive intro the first time it appears.

## Companion games

Each companion is a 1-to-4-minute game that rounds out the Daily Set; check each idea against existing games before building, since Nests and Stepping Stones build on known logic-puzzle types.

**Nests** (launch, Jan 2027)

- Board: 5 x 5 to 7 x 7 grid with numbered trees.
- Goal: place hidden nests so each tree's number equals how many nests it can see along its row and column, until another tree blocks the view.
- Extra rules: nests never touch each other, not even diagonally; nests never sit on a tree.
- One solution, solved by logic alone. Target time 2 to 4 minutes.

**Stepping Stones** (March 2027)

- Board: a pond of lily pads, each with a number.
- Goal: guide the frog from the start pad to the far bank. Each pad's number is exactly how far the frog must jump next, in a straight line (up, down, left, right).
- Daily twist on harder days: some pads sink after one use; some pads are hidden under leaves until the frog lands next to them.
- Target time 2 to 3 minutes.

**Who Was Here?** (May 2027)

- A zoomed-in illustration of an animal (fur, feather, eye, paw) zooms out with each wrong guess; 6 guesses.
- Type-ahead answer box with a fixed animal list, so spelling never blocks a correct guess.
- After solving: a short real fact and the animal's Journal card.
- Needs a library of original illustrations; plan 120+ animals before launch of this game (4 months of daily content). Never use photos you don't hold the rights to.

**Rules for every future game**

- Playable in under 5 minutes (except the flagship).
- Fits the forest world and feeds the Journal.
- Generated and verified by code, or built from a content library of at least 120 days.
- Kept only if at least 30% of players who try it play it again within 7 days.

## Streak system

Two streaks run side by side: the **Daily streak** (solve any one game that day) keeps casual players going, and the **Full Set streak** (solve every game in the set) rewards dedicated players. Snow Days protect both from a single missed day.

**Rules**

- A day runs from the player's local midnight to midnight. A puzzle counts for the date it belongs to.
- **Snow Days**: 1 free each week (refilled Monday), used automatically on a missed day. Earn 1 extra by finishing the Full Set 7 days in a row. Hold at most 3.
- **Repair**: once a calendar month, a broken streak can be restored by solving the missed day from the archive within 48 hours. Optionally unlocked by watching one rewarded video ad.
- Playing an archive day never adds to the streak except as a repair.
- Messages stay kind: "Fresh snow is waiting", never "You'll lose everything!"

**Milestones** (cosmetic rewards only)

| Streak | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| 3 days | First Journal badge |
| 7 days | +1 Snow Day and a rare animal card |
| 30 days | Northern-lights night sky theme |
| 100 days | Golden paw on the share card |
| 365 days | Legendary animal card and a "Year in the Wild" summary |

**Where it shows**

- Today page: paw icon with the Daily streak; small star with the Full Set streak.
- Stats page: a calendar where each day shows a footprint (partial) or golden footprint (full set).
- Share card: `🐾 Day 23`.

**Edge cases**

- Travel across time zones: the streak follows puzzle dates, so flying west never double-counts and flying east never breaks it.
- Device change or cleared browser: offer a backup code in Settings at launch; optional account sync later.
- New game added to the set: the Full Set streak only counts games that existed on that date.

## Retention systems

Players return daily for five reasons beyond the puzzles themselves: a collection to finish, events not to miss, friends to beat, a visible sense of improvement, and a reminder they chose.

| System | How it works | Why players return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest Journal | Every solved day adds an animal card (art + one real fact); all games feed it; rare animals on rare days | Completing the collection |
| Weekly rhythm | Easy Monday → hard Sunday Blizzard; Sunday stories end with "to be continued" solved on Monday | Curiosity and challenge |
| Events | Full-moon wolf packs, monthly rare animal, seasonal biome launches (snow, mud, sand, leaves) | Fear of missing something special |
| Friend leagues | Private groups by invite link (family, office, class of adults); daily times side by side; weekly winner badge | Friendly competition |
| Weekly summary | "You solved Night 22% faster this week"; best day; Journal cards earned | Visible progress |
| Reminders | Opt-in only: email or browser notification at a time the player picks, at most once a day | Habit trigger |
| Percentile | "Faster than 78% of players today" on each result | Daily self-test |
| Archive + practice | Past days and endless puzzles after the set is done | Extra play when hooked |

**Healthy-play guardrails**

- A clear "You're done for today" screen after the set, with tomorrow's countdown.
- No timers forced on players; times are shown but optional to share.
- Reminders are off by default and easy to turn off.

## Family and Classroom modes

Kids join through the adults around them: parents through Family mode, teachers through a free Classroom page. No engagement nudges, streak pressure or reminders are aimed at children, and kids' pages carry no personalized ads.

**Family mode**

- A parent creates a family group on their own device; family members are just display names chosen by the parent (no child accounts, no child emails).
- Anyone can pick the **Cub** level (3 x 3 or 4 x 4 boards) without being asked their age.
- A family scoreboard shows each member's result for the day, kept on the parent's device.
- The parent's device keeps the streak; the parent decides if reminders are on.

**Classroom mode** (`/classroom`)

- A large-screen "Puzzle of the Day" teachers can project and solve together with the class.
- Each day: one Cub or Morning-level puzzle, one real animal fact and one discussion question ("Why does a rabbit leave prints in pairs?").
- A printable worksheet version each week.
- No accounts, no data collected from students, no ads (or only non-personalized ads) on these pages.

**Why this helps the business**: teachers and parents share the hub with other adults, who play the regular Daily Set where normal ads run, and the hub earns a family-safe reputation that ad networks value.

**Compliance notes** (confirm with a lawyer before launch): tag `/classroom` and any kids' pages as child-directed for Google ads; keep the main hub positioned for adults and families; follow COPPA (US), the UK Children's Code, GDPR (EU) and India's data protection law for any child-facing feature.

## Hints, scoring and difficulty tuning

Hints help without punishing, scores reward clean solving, and difficulty is tuned weekly from real solve times rather than guesswork.

**Hints** (same three steps in every logic game)

1. Nudge: highlight a cell or area where the next logical step is.
2. Step: place one correct step for one animal (or one nest, one jump).
3. Reveal: show one animal's full path and hideout.

Hints are free and unlimited; they show on the share card ("1 hint") and keep the solve out of the day's speed percentile.

**Score shown on results**

- Time (pauses when the tab is hidden).
- Checks used and hints used.
- A paw rating: 3 paws = no hints and no failed checks, 2 paws = up to 1 hint or 2 failed checks, 1 paw = anything more.

**Weekly difficulty tuning**

| Signal | Healthy range | If outside the range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Median Morning time | 1 to 2 min | Shift the Morning difficulty band |
| Median Night time | 8 to 12 min | Adjust covered cells and crossings |
| Quit rate per level | Under 15% (Night under 25%) | Lower the band or improve the tutorial for that mechanic |
| Hint use on Morning | Under 10% of solves | Morning is too hard; simplify |
| Sunday completion | Over 50% of Sunday starters | Cap Blizzard size |

The generator's difficulty score is recalibrated monthly against these real numbers, so "Night" always feels like Night.
